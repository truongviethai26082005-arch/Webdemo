## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a premium, authoritative, yet approachable environment tailored for a modern international English learning center. Designed for ambitious professionals, university candidates, and discerning parents, the system evokes trust, academic excellence, intellectual clarity, and global mobility.

The aesthetic leans into **Corporate / Modern Editorial**:
- **Structured Trust:** Grounded by authoritative deep naval blues that project permanence and institutional prestige, banishing the hyper-casual chaos often found in mass-market language apps.
- **Micro-Energies:** High-warmth marigold accents (`#F5A623`) are deployed with absolute surgical restraint—reserved solely for high-conversion actions, live statuses, and milestone markers.
- **Spatial Generosity:** Ample whitespace, crisp hairline structures, and subtle icy tinting replace visual noise, letting curriculum pathways, faculty credentials, and outcome metrics breathe effortlessly.

## Colors

The palette communicates collegiate prestige balanced with clean digital legibility. The system deliberately deprecates high-saturation orange backgrounds, elevating deep navy as the commanding core tone.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Navy (`#0C2356`):** The institutional core. Used for master headers, solid high-status cards, active navigational anchors, and authoritative typography.
- **Secondary Academic Cobalt (`#1E429F`):** Used for hover states on primary components, secondary interactive buttons, key iconography, and subheadings. An intermediate tone (`#16367C`) supports subtle linear fills and structural framing.
- **Tertiary Warm Amber (`#F5A623`):** Strictly a micro-accent. Reserved for high-value CTA pill buttons, conversion badges, star ratings, active cohort indicators, and key numeric metrics. An active pressed variant sits at `#E67E22`.
- **Canvas & Neutrals:**
  - `Surface Base`: Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) for core content cards and clarity panels.
  - `Surface Icy Mist`: (`#F4F7FB` to `#F8FAFC`) for subtle alternating landing page sections.
  - `Border Hairline`: Crisp cold-grey (`#E2E8F0` / `#D8E2EE`) for card boundaries.
  - `Text Primary`: Deep Ink (`#101828`) ensuring WCAG AAA accessibility across light surfaces.
  - `Text Muted`: Cool Slate (`#475467`) for secondary curriculum descriptions.

## Typography

The typography pairs **Plus Jakarta Sans** for display geometry with **Inter** for sustained technical and academic legibility.

- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Headlines, Stats, Labels):** Provides contemporary polish and geometric balance without feeling cold. Its distinctive letterforms anchor hero statements, course module titles, and credential callouts.
- **Inter (Body, Descriptions, Form Fields):** Provides neutral, utilitarian balance. Designed with high x-height for clear reading of curriculum syllabi, class schedule tables, and tuition overviews.
- **Discipline in Scaling:** All display sizes transition smoothly into designated mobile variants below the 768px threshold to eliminate unnatural hyphenation or screen-edge clipping.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on a 12-column grid system capped at a maximum container width of `1280px`, ensuring optimal line lengths and balanced framing on ultra-wide desktop monitors.

### Responsive Breakpoint Rules
- **Desktop (1024px - 1440px+):** 12 columns, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters, and a minimum canvas margin of `3rem` (48px). Section vertical padding uses `space-3xl` (96px) to maintain breathing room.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, `1.25rem` (20px) gutters, and `2rem` (32px) margins. Grid cards reflow from 3 or 4 columns down to 2 columns.
- **Mobile (&lt; 768px):** 4 columns, `1rem` (16px) gutters, and `1.25rem` (20px) outer margin. Multi-column course tiers collapse into clean vertically stacked cards.

### Layout Principles
- **Hero Staging:** A 7/5 or 6/6 split grid where primary value propositions sit left, and dynamic photography (students, international faculty, real classroom environment) is nested right inside a precision-clipped card container with an offset icy-blue accent backdrop.
- **Generous Grouping:** Sub-elements inside cards strictly observe an 8px base rhythm (`space-sm` to `space-xl`), keeping course metadata tightly bundled while distinct courses remain well separated.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established primarily through **tonal layers and subtle diffused ambient shadows** rather than heavy drop-shadows or stark borders.

- **Level 0 (Flat):** Base surface for content canvases (`#FFFFFF` or icy mist `#F4F7FB`).
- **Level 1 (Resting Cards & Course Containers):** Uses a razor-thin border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) paired with a whisper shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(12, 35, 86, 0.04), 0 4px 16px -4px rgba(12, 35, 86, 0.06)`. The navy tint inside the shadow preserves atmospheric temperature.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover & Featured Tiers):** Applied when hovering over course cards or interactive roadmap nodes: `0 12px 28px -6px rgba(12, 35, 86, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(12, 35, 86, 0.04)`. Card transitions slightly elevate by `-2px` along the Y-axis.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays, Floating Dock):** Used for sticky consultation request drawers and floating contact triggers: `0 20px 36px -8px rgba(12, 35, 86, 0.18), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(12, 35, 86, 0.08)`.
- **Glassmorphic Sub-layering:** Used exclusively for sticky top navigation bars and persistent badge tags. Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` with a `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and a subtle bottom border in `rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **Rounded** posture (`roundedness: 2`), balancing collegiate dignity with modern friendly software design.

- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Badges):** Feature an `8px` (`0.5rem`) radius, offering a crisp, tactile, and dependable footprint.
- **Cards and Course Containers:** Feature a `16px` (`1rem`, `rounded-lg`) corner radius, softening the boundaries of rich photography and dense course syllabi.
- **Hero Image Masks & Highlight Modules:** Feature a `24px` (`1.5rem`, `rounded-xl`) corner radius to command visual attention naturally.
- **Micro-Badges & Floating Actions:** Mini indicator chips and the bottom-right floating speed-dial utilize a complete pill format (`9999px`) to distinguish them from rectangular page architecture.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Accent CTA (High Conversion):** Surface in Amber `#F5A623`, text in `#0C2356` (semi-bold), with a subtle hover transition to `#E67E22`. Used exclusively for primary conversions like "Book Free Level Test" or "Enroll Now".
- **Primary Navy CTA:** Solid deep navy `#0C2356` background, crisp white text, transitioning to `#1E429F` on hover. Used for section-level standard actions.
- **Ghost / Outline:** Transparent fill, `1.5px` border in `#1E429F`, navy text. On hover, fills with `#F4F7FB`.
- **Button Padding:** Vertical `12px`, horizontal `24px` for standard controls; vertical `16px`, horizontal `32px` for hero buttons.

### Chips & Academic Badges
- **Program Badges:** Compact pill tags (`padding: 4px 12px`, `font-size: 11px`, uppercase, bold).
- **Default State:** Subtle icy navy wash (`background: #EEF4FC`, `color: #1E429F`).
- **Urgent / Milestone State:** Amber glow (`background: #FEF6E7`, `color: #B45309`, optional `1px solid rgba(245, 166, 35, 0.3)`).

### Cards (Course & Faculty Modules)
- **Structure:** Clean white surface (`#FFFFFF`), `1px solid #E2E8F0` border, `16px` border-radius.
- **Card Hierarchy:** Header holds image/tag, body contains title (`headline-sm`) followed by target outcomes, footer includes cohort start dates and pricing/action link separated by a hairline border.
- **Featured Tier:** Outlined with a top accent bar (`3px solid #F5A623`) or a refined full outline in `#1E429F`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Base Style:** 48px height, `8px` corner radius, `#FFFFFF` surface, border `1.5px solid #D8E2EE`.
- **Focus State:** Border shifts cleanly to `#1E429F` with an ambient ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(30, 66, 159, 0.15)`. No orange outlines.
- **Labels:** Crisp 13px bold labels in Navy (`#0C2356`) positioned 6px above the field.

### Floating Contact & Quick-Assist Action
- **Positioning:** Fixed at bottom-right viewport (`bottom: 32px`, `right: 32px`, z-index: 100).
- **Design:** Elevated circular dock (56px diameter) or pill housing high-frequency channels (Hotline, Zalo/WhatsApp, Direct Consultation). Primary anchor features Deep Navy `#0C2356` with an Amber micro-dot notification (`#F5A623`) indicating immediate counselor availability.

### Metrics & Stat Counters
- **Display:** Large numerical figures set in `Plus Jakarta Sans` Bold (36px–48px) colored in Deep Navy `#0C2356` with metric symbols (`+`, `%`, `Band`) tipped in Amber `#F5A623`.
- **Caption:** Accompanying descriptions in `#475467` 14px regular typography positioned beneath the value.
